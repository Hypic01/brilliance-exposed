The Exhibition

A mirror for a community that has always been extraordinary — and a record for those who come next.

Our Mission

A Movement in Black & White

Brilliance Exposed is an international photographic exhibition honoring Black-presenting professionals whose work quietly shapes the world we live in — in Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics.

Each honoree is captured in a single, deliberate black-and-white portrait. We chose that form on purpose. Stripped of color, stripped of context, what remains is presence. What remains is the person. When a young visitor recognizes a face on the wall and hears, for the first time, that the woman whose mathematics sits underneath every GPS signal looks like their grandmother — that is the moment the exhibition is for.

Why this, and why now

The history of STEAM is full of names that were erased, delayed, or simply never recorded. Brilliance Exposed is an act of recognition and an act of archival: a refusal to let that erasure continue. The exhibition travels alongside an interview series, a book, and a growing public archive of primary-source stories told in each honoree’s own voice.

When we see ourselves reflected in excellence, we begin to believe in the possibility of our own brilliance. This exhibition is a mirror for a community that has always been extraordinary.

How the exhibition travels

The 2026 season opens at the Charles H. Wright Museum of African American History in Detroit, makes its international debut in Paris at Galerie Brilliance in June, and closes the summer with a private reception at the Tiffany & Co. flagship in New York City. Additional venues are being confirmed for the 2027 tour.

The Creators

The People Behind the Lens

Alexis Dixon

Founder & Project Visionary

Alexis Dixon conceived Brilliance Exposed as an act of public recognition — a traveling archive of the Black-presenting minds whose work shapes STEAM. She leads the curatorial direction, the international touring schedule, and the ongoing honoree recruitment.

Photography Team

Portrait Series

Each portrait in the series is captured on location in a controlled low-key studio setup, rendered in full-frame black and white to focus entirely on the subject. Lighting and composition follow a consistent visual grammar across the collection to unify the traveling exhibition.

Design for America — UCSD

Website & Digital Experience

A seven-person student team from Design for America at UC San Diego built this companion website over the Winter 2026 quarter. The team combined user research, competitive audits, information architecture, and front-end development to bring the exhibition online.

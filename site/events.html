“Brilliance Within”

Charles H. Wright Museum of African American History

From 3.14.26 – 5.21.26

The inaugural exhibition of Brilliance Exposed opens at the Charles H. Wright Museum of African American History in Detroit, Michigan — one of the largest institutions in the world dedicated to the African American experience.

Featuring the full 2026 portrait series alongside the premiere of the interview documentary segments, the Detroit installation sets the visual and narrative standard for the international tour that follows.

Fund the Next Portrait

Every dollar carries the work to a new honoree, a new interview, and a new venue. This is how the record is built.

Brilliance Exposed is a donor-supported initiative. Every contribution directly funds new portraits, video interviews, archival infrastructure, and the travel that brings the exhibition to museums and galleries around the world.

Contributions are processed through our fiscal partner and are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. For major gifts, endowment conversations, corporate sponsorships, or legacy giving, please contact Alexis directly — we would be honored to walk you through the impact in person.

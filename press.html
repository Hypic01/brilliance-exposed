About the Exhibition

Brilliance Exposed is an international photographic exhibition celebrating Black-presenting pioneers of Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts and Mathematics. Founded by Alexis Dixon and photographed by award-winning humanitarian photographer Michele Zousmer, the series will grow to fifty museum-scale black-and-white portraits, each paired with the honoree’s own story recorded in a filmed interview.

The exhibition opened at the Charles H. Wright Museum of African American History in Detroit (March 14 – June 21, 2026), made its international debut at Galerie Brilliance in Paris (June 1 – August 15, 2026), and continues to New York in fall 2026 with a reception at the Tiffany & Co. Fifth Avenue flagship.

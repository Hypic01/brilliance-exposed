About the Exhibition

From Me, to We,
to All Humanity

Brilliance Exposed is an international photographic exhibition celebrating Black-presenting pioneers of Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts and Mathematics — fifty museum-scale portraits, each paired with the honoree’s own story.

Our Mission

Inspiring individuals to discover, acknowledge and expose the brilliance within.

Our Vision

Inviting the ever-curious to co-create and realize their collective brilliance — as an offering to all humanity.

The Exhibition

The Idea Behind
the Series

The individuals in Brilliance Exposed defied historical barriers and pushed the boundaries of human knowledge — proof that excellence is not inherited privilege, but the courage to challenge the unknown.

Presented through a fine-art lens in museum and gallery spaces, their portraits change how young people — particularly those long underrepresented in STEAM — see themselves in relation to knowledge and leadership. These portraits do not merely celebrate the past; they invite engagement, discussion, and participation.

Each portrait becomes a mirror — an invitation to see yourself as an active contributor to the future.

The Creators

The Creators

Alexis Dixon

Visionary & Founder

Born in Guyana, Alexis Dixon has spent two decades practicing humanistic conflict resolution — transforming conflict into connection through deep listening and empathy. He carries the legacy of his father, Sir Eric Matthew Gairy, Grenada’s first Prime Minister, who led his nation to independence.

“We are spiritual giants, yet we behave as spiritual dwarfs.” — words from Archbishop Desmond Tutu, spoken to Alexis in person, that ground his work to this day.

Brilliance Exposed is the bold extension of that work: a declaration of empowerment, a reclaiming of space, and a tribute to the boundless potential of human creativity and intellect.

Michele Zousmer

Humanitarian Photographer

Michele Zousmer is an internationally recognized, award-winning humanitarian photographer whose portraits reveal both interiority and strength — each subject rendered with reverence and clarity.

Her black-and-white portraiture translates achievement into presence, and intellect into image — an encounter at once intimate and monumental.

A trusted colleague of Alexis Dixon for more than a decade, Zousmer shot the entire Brilliance Exposed series.

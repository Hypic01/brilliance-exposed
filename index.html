01

The Mission

Brilliance is not gained.
It is revealed.

What if you never saw yourself in successful roles or achievements? For millions of young people, that is not a hypothetical.

So we photograph Black pioneers of science, technology, engineering, arts and mathematics — and hang their portraits where the world cannot look away.

See It

Fifty museum-scale portraits by humanitarian photographer Michele Zousmer — scientists, engineers, artists and mathematicians rendered as the icons they are.

Hear It

Every honoree sits for an interview. Their stories — the turning points, the closed doors, the breakthroughs — play alongside the portraits.

Stand In It

The exhibition tours museums and galleries worldwide, so a student anywhere can stand before a portrait and think: that could be me.
